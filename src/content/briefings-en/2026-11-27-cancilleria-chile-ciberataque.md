**In one line:** the Chilean foreign ministry says it contained an attack, but without saying what it reached, the public response is only half done.

## What happened

On 25 November 2026, Chile's Ministry of Foreign Affairs confirmed, in a written reply to Radio Bío Bío and in a statement reproduced by Emol, that an episode "weeks ago" affected its institutional systems and that it was "detected and neutralized". The ministry says its platforms are operating normally, with no service interruption, and that its prevention, detection and response capabilities are being updated and strengthened.

The ministry did not disclose the origin of the attack, the access vector or whether any information was compromised. Congressman Nelson Venegas announced he will formally ask the ministry about the scope of the breach and the measures taken. Press reports, not denied by the ministry, say Navy Naval Intelligence personnel supported the response.

## Why it matters

Containing an incident and knowing what it reached are different questions. A foreign ministry holds diplomatic communications and data on citizens abroad; without a map of which repositories were within the intrusion's reach, there is no way to notify those affected or to assess the national security risk the congressman pointed to.

## Risk reading

| Vector | Public evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Intrusion into institutional systems | Ministry confirms a cyberattack detected and neutralized | High |
| Unknown scope | Ministry does not say whether information was compromised | High |
| Transparency | Details only after pressure from the press and Congress | Medium |

## What to do this week

1. Make sure the incident playbook includes an explicit "data scope" step: which repositories the compromised account or system could read (owner: CISO).
2. Keep an up-to-date inventory of repositories with sensitive data and of who has access to each one (owner: data governance).
3. Prepare a public communication template that states what is known and what is still under investigation about the affected data (owner: legal and communications).
